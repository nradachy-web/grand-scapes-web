# Mundy Township, MI: research notes

Read 2026-11-21. The township site (mundytwp-mi.gov) was read live. Wikipedia only for general geography.

## The place
- Charter Township of Mundy, Genesee County. Organized 1837, named for Lieutenant Governor Edward Mundy. Population 15,281 (2020 census). Area 36.1 square miles. Unincorporated communities: Rankin (originally Mundy Centre), just west of the US-23 exit on Grand Blanc Road, where the township offices are; and Mundy, at Linden and Baldwin Roads in the southwest. Part of the survey township between Jennings and Torrey Roads was annexed by Flint for Bishop International Airport. "Roads are administrated by the Genesee County Road Commission." ZIP codes 48430, 48439, 48442, 48473, 48507. Police: Metro Police Authority of Genesee County.
  Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mundy_Township,_Michigan (read via the Wikipedia API, 2026-11-21)
- Megasite: a 970 acre site bounded by Elms, Maple, Linden and Hill Roads, marketed by the state; the Sandisk project was cancelled July 16, 2025. Source: Wikipedia, as above.
- Township history page: "Today, Mundy Township consists of a mix of commercial, small industry development, but the majority of our land is made up of residential and farm land." Close to I-75, I-69, US-23 and Bishop International Airport.
  Source: https://www.mundytwp-mi.gov/about/history.php (read live 2026-11-21)
- Township hall: 3478 Mundy Ave, Swartz Creek, MI 48473, (810) 655-4631. Source: site footer, 2026-11-21.

## Water
- Swartz Creek's east branch "flows through a number of small lakes in Fenton Township, taking a turn northwards to flow through Mundy Township", then along the west side of Bishop Airport. East branch tributaries: Indian Creek (joins at Cook Road east of US-23), Dawe Creek, Seaver Drain (Cook Road west of Fenton Road), Brewer Drain (between Brewer Road and US-23, south of Reid Road and north of Grand Blanc Road).
  Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swartz_Creek (read via the Wikipedia API, 2026-11-21)

## Lots (Mundy Township Zoning Ordinance 23-123-Z, amendments effective 10-18-24)
- Schedule of Regulations: RA Residential/Agriculture, 1 acre without sewer, 20,000 sq ft with sewer. RSA Residential Suburban Agricultural, 1 acre without sewer, 14,000 sq ft with sewer. RM-1 Residential Urban, 12,000 sq ft, 10,000 in cluster subdivisions. Lot coverage by buildings capped at 30% in these districts.
- Clear vision zone (Section 4.12): at corners of intersecting roads, a triangle 50 feet along each right of way line where no obstruction is allowed from 2 to 8 feet above the street (limited tree exceptions).
- Parking lots over 20 spaces: at least 5% of the surface used for interior landscaping "for purposes of water absorption, snow storage, and for installation of plant materials".
- Hill Road Development Corridor Overlay (C-3A): a 2,000 foot wide strip along Hill Road "extending from Fenton Road west to the centerline of Jennings Road".
  Source: https://www.mundytwp-mi.gov/10-17-2024%20Mundy%20Zoning%20Ordinance%2023-123-Z.pdf (linked from https://www.mundytwp-mi.gov/ordinances/index.php, read live 2026-11-21)

## Soils (USDA NRCS Soil Data Access, Genesee County survey MI049)
Point samples at 8 spots confirmed inside Mundy Township by the Census geocoder:
- Del Rey silt loam, 2 to 6 percent slopes: 3 points. Somewhat poorly drained, hydrologic group C/D, "Fine, illitic" family (clayey), on till and lake plains.
- Conover loam, 0 to 4 percent slopes: 3 points. Somewhat poorly drained, C/D.
- Sloan silt loam: 1 point. Very poorly drained, formed in alluvium on flood plains.
- Celina-Conover loams, 2 to 6 percent slopes: 1 point. Celina moderately well drained.
  Source: https://SDMDataAccess.sc.egov.usda.gov/Tabular/post.rest (read 2026-11-21); place check via https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/ (2026-11-21)
- What it means: heavy, slow draining ground over most of the township; wet bottoms along the creek and drains. Lawns stay soft in spring; new lots graded from subsoil drain poorly.

## Roads and snow (Genesee County Road Commission)
- "Roads are ranked in terms of priority based on traffic volume. The first priority is expressways. Next is main streets and third is local roads. Subdivision streets are the final priority." More than 1,600 miles of roads. "We cannot give a specific time as to when a certain street will be plowed."
- Mailboxes: can be damaged by plowing in heavy snow; owners "are encouraged to inspect their mailboxes/supports before the winter season each year". (The FAQ also states a reimbursement amount; not printed.)
- Right of way: "A permit is required to install anything within the public right of way." Listed hazards: rocks, "solid flower garden borders of decorative timbers, mail boxes (especially those installed on large brick, concrete, or steel posts)", fences, trees, bushes; they can block sight distance at intersections and driveways.
  Source: https://www.gcrc.org/about/frequently-asked-questions (read live 2026-11-21)
- MCL 257.677a(4): "A person shall not deposit, or cause to be deposited, snow, ice or slush on any roadway or highway."
  Source: https://www.legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-257-677a (read live 2026-11-21)
- No township sidewalk snow ordinance found (the Municode library would not load). Left out.

## Trash, yard waste, burning
- Republic Services. "All residential curbside garbage/recycling/yard waste in Mundy Township is collected on Tuesdays." Carts out by 6:00 a.m.
- "From April through November: All yard waste must be contained in biodegradable Kraft paper bags or placed in clearly marked 30 gallon can. No plastic bags accepted. Branches and twigs must be smaller than 2 inches in diameter and must be placed in bundles no larger than 2 ft by 4 ft long or 50 pounds per Kraft paper bag or bundle. NOT considered yard waste: Tree trunks, stumps, large bushes, construction or demolition debris."
- Monthly dumping permit at Citizens Disposal Landfill, 2361 W. Grand Blanc Road: "No hazardous materials accepted or yard waste, trees, limbs, bushes etc."
  Source: https://www.mundytwp-mi.gov/newsdetail_T3_R99.php (read live 2026-11-21)
- Burn permits (Mundy Township Fire Department): "ONLY LOGS, LIMBS, STUMPS AND BRUSH may be burned. Household or commercial refuse, lumber, leaf rakings, ground cover, fence rows, ditch lines, etc. MAY NOT BE BURNED." Permits valid three days; the fire must be out half an hour before dark; permits from the Fire Hall at Hill and Jennings Road or online.
  Source: https://www.mundytwp-mi.gov/departments/fire_department/burn_permits.php (read live 2026-11-21)

## Horticulture (MSU Extension), used on Mundy pages
- Mowing: current standards 2 to 3.75 inches; one third rule; every four to five days in fast spring growth; clippings go back on the lawn. https://www.canr.msu.edu/uploads/resources/pdfs/mowing_lawn_turf_(e0013).pdf (Internet Archive copy of 2025-11-11)
- Turning riding mowers on wet or saturated soil can compact it or damage the turf. https://www.canr.msu.edu/news/how_to_care_for_rapidly_growing_lawns (read 2026-11-21)
- Leaves: highest deck setting, dewy not soaking, keep the layer under 3 to 4 inches; last cut no more than one notch lower. https://www.canr.msu.edu/news/final_fall_turf_chores (read 2026-11-21)
- Mulch: 2 to 3 inches; 1 inch top dressing; no mulch volcanoes. https://www.canr.msu.edu/news/mulching_landscape_beds_faqs (Internet Archive copy of 2026-05-13)
- Salt: damage from spray and buildup beside drives and walks; calcium based de-icers safer near plants. https://www.canr.msu.edu/news/salt_damage_on_landscape_plants (Internet Archive copy of 2026-04-18)
- Pruning: spring bloomers right after bloom. https://www.canr.msu.edu/news/pruning_spring_flowering_shrubs (Internet Archive copy of 2026-01-24)
