# Research: Linden (City of Linden, with Fenton Township around it)

For `src/content/cities/linden.json` and the Linden town pages (lawn-mowing, snow-removal, landscaping, fall-cleanup).
All read 2026-11-21. Quotes are exact. The City of Linden is a small city; the land around it is Fenton Charter Township (see `docs/research/fenton.md` for township and Road Commission sources), with Argentine Township to the west.

## Geography and history

City of Linden, About Linden. https://www.lindenmi.us/about-linden
- "Forming around our present downtown at the intersection of Bridge and Broad, the Village of Linden was established in 1871, with structures dating back to 1840. We have grown from a village into a well-established city (1988), home to a population of about 4,000 residents".
- "The heart of our city is the Linden Mills building, an historic treasure sitting on the bank of the Shiawassee River". "Across the River from the Mill building sits a picturesque park that is home to the Linden Mill Pond Gazebo."
- "Eagles Wooden Park and Playground, which bounds the River, is surrounded by an expanse of woods".
- "Linden County Park consists of 135 acres, including a 1.1 mile walking trail and bike path. The path connects to the City’s sidewalk system".
- The city site lists a Historic District Commission.
Wikipedia, "Linden, Michigan" (general geography only). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linden,_Michigan
- Area 2.43 square miles. "Linden is a lowland area typical of southern Michigan consisting of marsh, swamplands, previously dredged marl bottom lakes and surrounded by the similar geographic area of Fenton Township."
Wikipedia, "Shiawassee River". https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shiawassee_River
- "It flows through Holly, Fenton, Linden, Byron, Corunna, Owosso, and Chesaning."

## Soil

USDA NRCS Soil Data Access (SSURGO, survey area MI049), queried 2026-11-21, 6 by 6 point grid over the city.
- Wawasee loam, 2 to 6 percent slopes (8) and 6 to 12 percent slopes (4); Conover loam, 0 to 4 percent slopes (7); Carlisle and Linwood mucks (6); Boyer loamy sand, 0 to 12 percent slopes (7 in three classes); Gilford sandy loam, Sloan silt loam, Lake beaches, Water (1 each).
- Drainage: Wawasee "Well drained"; Boyer "Well drained" (sandy); Conover "Somewhat poorly drained"; Carlisle and Linwood "Very poorly drained" (muck).
- Use on the site: rolling, well drained loam and sand on the higher ground with wet muck in the low spots, about one sample point in six. Stated generally.

## City of Linden: trash, yard waste, parking, burning

City of Linden, Trash and Recycling. https://www.lindenmi.us/trash
- "Residential curbside refuse and recycling services for the City of Linden are provided by Priority Waste Services. Trash collection is weekly on Wednesday".
- "Please note, yard waste collection begins in April, with the final collection being December 2."
Priority Waste, Linden brochure (linked from the city page). https://irp.cdn-website.com/0b76c777/files/uploaded/Linden_Brochure_Final+%281%29.pdf
- "Acceptable Yard Waste: grass clippings, leaves, shrubs, and brush trimmings (branches 2-6" inches in diameter must bundled and tied, and no longer than 4 feet in length and less than 50 pounds)".
- "All compost material must be placed in paper bags or clearly marked containers with a maxi- mum capacity of 35 gallons and weighing less than 50 pounds".
- Unacceptable includes dirt, rocks, root balls and oversized branches.
- 2026 calendar: "Collection of compost will occur Wednesdays from April 1 - December 2, 2026."
City of Linden, Fall 2026 newsletter (PDF). https://irp.cdn-website.com/0b76c777/files/uploaded/FALL+2026.pdf
- "Yard Waste Collection ends December 2".
- "No On-Street Parking November 1 - March 31". The hours in the PDF text are garbled ("2:30 a.m. to 6:00 p.m."), so the site states the dates only and says to check the city for the hours.
City of Linden, Residents and Most Requested pages. https://www.lindenmi.us/residents
- "Burn Permit" is a most requested city form. The form PDF is a scan; its terms were not read, so the site says only that the city issues burn permits and to ask the city.
- No city leaf vacuum program found.

## Roads around the city

Fenton Township roads around Linden are plowed by the Genesee County Road Commission, with subdivision streets last; sources and quotes in `docs/research/fenton.md` (Fenton Township snow page, capture 2026-04-09; GCRC Winter Maintenance Policy).
- Linden Road is named by the township as a primary road.
