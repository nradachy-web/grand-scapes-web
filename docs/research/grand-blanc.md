# Research: Grand Blanc (City of Grand Blanc and Grand Blanc Township)

For `src/content/cities/grand-blanc.json` (the hub; the service pages are the main pages for Grand Blanc).
All read 2026-11-21 unless a capture date is given. Quotes are exact. Two governments: the City of Grand Blanc (small, surrounded by the township) and the Charter Township of Grand Blanc. Their rules differ, so the hub says which is which.

## Geography

Wikipedia, "Grand Blanc, Michigan" and "Grand Blanc Township, Michigan" (general geography only).
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Blanc,_Michigan
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Blanc_Township,_Michigan
- City: "The city is surrounded by Grand Blanc Township but is administratively autonomous." Area 3.63 square miles.
- Township: 32.7 square miles. "Thread Creek, a tributary of the Flint River, flows from south to north through the township."
- City and township voters both rejected consolidation in 2006.
- Not used: population figures (the article gives two different 2020 numbers for the city).

## Soil

USDA NRCS Soil Data Access (SSURGO, Genesee County survey area MI049), queried 2026-11-21 at https://SDMDataAccess.sc.egov.usda.gov/Tabular/post.rest . Map units sampled on a 6 by 6 grid of points over each area.
- City of Grand Blanc (36 points): Celina-Conover loams, 2 to 6 percent slopes (16), Conover loam, 0 to 4 percent slopes (8), Udorthents and Udipsamments (4, disturbed or made ground), single hits of Wawasee, Metea, Perrin, Sloan silt loam (floodplain), Spinks-Oakville.
- Grand Blanc Township (36 points): Conover loam, 0 to 4 percent slopes (16 plus 1), Celina-Conover loams (4), Wawasee loam (2), Sloan silt loam (2 plus 1 "occasionally flooded"), mucks (Houghton and Adrian 2, Edwards 1, Carlisle and Linwood 1), Brookston, Sebewa, Miami, Metamora, Selfridge.
- Drainage classes from the component table: Conover "Somewhat poorly drained"; Celina "Moderately well drained"; Wawasee "Well drained"; Houghton, Adrian, Carlisle, Linwood mucks "Very poorly drained".
- Use on the site: mostly heavy, slow draining loam; wet in spring; low mucky spots. Stated generally ("much of Grand Blanc sits on heavy loam that drains slowly"), never as a lab result for one yard.

## City of Grand Blanc: waste, branches, leaves

City of Grand Blanc, Waste Collection page.
https://cityofgrandblanc.com/our_residents/city_departments/public_works/waste_collection.php (resolves to cityofgrandblancmi.gov)
- "Republic Services is the waste collection company for the City of Grand Blanc." Garbage pickup is Monday.
- "Branches tied and bundled in 4-foot lengths will be picked up by Republic Services every week on trash day April through November."
- Clean-Up Week: "a bi-annual event held each Spring and Fall". 2026 spring: May 4 to 8. 2026 fall: October 26 to 30. "Branches 4” or less in diameter, that are NOT tied or bundled, will be collected during Cleanup Week ... by the City Department of Public Works with the City chipper. Please place these branches to the curb the Sunday night before the clean-up week ... as DPW will not be able to go back once a street has been completed."

City of Grand Blanc, "2026 Leaf Collection Schedule" (PDF linked from that page).
https://www.cityofgrandblancmi.gov/2026%20Leaf%20Collection%20Information.pdf
- Two zones. "Zone # 2: Week of November 9th", "Zone # 1: Week of November 16th".
- "Leaves must be raked to the curb of the street by 7:00 A.M. on Monday morning of the week your zone is to be collected."
- "DO NOT RAKE LEAVES INTO THE STREET." "If brush or grass clippings are in the pile, they will not be collected."
- "After the truck has gone by your house, DO NOT rake leaves to the curb. Remaining leaves MUST be bagged for regular pickup by Republic Services. Bagged leaf pickup ends the last Monday of November – November 23, 2026."
- "If it rains or snows for several days, leaves will be wet and the City machine will not operate."
- Use on the site: dates change every year, so the pages say "the city vacuums leaves from the curb by zone in November" and "check the city's leaf page".

## Grand Blanc Township: yard waste

Grand Blanc Township, Garbage Collection. https://grandblanctwpmi.gov/276/Garbage-Collection
- Hauler Emterra; "The garbage collection for all of Grand Blanc will be on Wednesdays."
- "Yard waste includes grass clippings, leaves, garden debris, and small branches/shrubbery less than 4 feet in length and 2 inches in diameter (must be bundled and tied). Less than 50 pounds per bundle. Yard waste will be accepted in brown paper lawn and garden bags or loose in trash cans up to 30 gallons clearly marked with a large "X" and less than 50 pounds per container/bag."
- Not accepted includes straw or hay, apples, logs and stumps, dirt and rocks, pinecones, nuts, "Mulch of any kind", compost in plastic bags.
- "Compost Season begins the first full week in April and runs through the last full week in November."
- No township curbside leaf vacuum program found.

## Grand Blanc Township: burning

Grand Blanc Township Fire, Outdoor Burning Guidelines. https://grandblanctwpmi.gov/262/Outdoor-Burning-Guidelines
- "Outdoor Burning Regulations coincide with our Township Yard Waste Collection Schedule." "We DO NOT issue burn permits."
- "Only logs, tree limbs, clean lumber, and brush may be burned." "Burning of leaves, grass clippings, or any other materials is prohibited."
- "Wind speed must be less than 10 miles an hour for outdoor burning."

## Grand Blanc Township: snow on the roads

Grand Blanc Township DPW, Snow Removal. https://grandblanctwpmi.gov/203/Snow-Removal
- "The DPW removes snow from 106 miles of residential streets throughout the Township. The Genesee County Road Commission maintains all major streets (Dort Highway, Saginaw, Baldwin, Holly, etc.)"
- "Snow removal crews are dispatched after streets realize 4" or more of accumulation." (The township's own rule for its trucks. The site never gives a trigger for Grand Scapes.)
- "Salt is only applied to intersections, hills, and dangerous curves."
- "Cul de sacs are plowed after the large trucks have been through ... smaller pick-up trucks must come back through the same day or sometimes the day after".
- "There are times where a couple of days after the storm, we have to go back out to widen roads with larger plows and we may plow snow back into your driveway."
- "Don't put your garbage cans in the road when we will be out plowing. Leave them in your driveway a couple of feet from the road."
- "Please do not shovel your snow back into the road. ... This is also against the law and violators can be prosecuted."
- Mailboxes: "Report damage to the Township immediately if your mailbox is hit"; Mailbox Replacement Policy adopted October 9, 2014. A "Mailbox/Yard Damage Report - Snow Event" form is on the township site.
DPW page: https://www.grandblanctwpmi.gov/187/Department-of-Public-Works-DPW
- "the DPW performs snow and ice removal services for residential (public subdivision) streets within the Township".

## State law on snow in the road

MCL 257.677a, Michigan Vehicle Code. https://www.legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-257-677a
- "(4) A person shall not deposit, or cause to be deposited, snow, ice or slush on any roadway or highway."
- Use on the site: "Michigan law says snow cannot be pushed onto the road", so we stack it on the yard side of the drive.

## Genesee County Road Commission (township major roads)

GCRC FAQ. https://www.gcrc.org/about/frequently-asked-questions
- Plow priority: "The first priority is expressways. Next is main streets and third is local roads. Subdivision streets are the final priority."
- Right of way: "A permit is required to install anything within the public right of way." Hazards listed include rocks and "solid flower garden borders of decorative timbers".
GCRC Winter Maintenance Policy (PDF). https://www.gcrc.org/forms-documents/forms/policies/240-winter-maintenance-policy/file
- "Works crews usually plow half of a road to one side and then the other. This means snow is pushed into driveways. This is unavoidable."
