# Davison: research notes

Covers the City of Davison and Davison Township (Genesee County), northeast of Grand Blanc. All sources read 2026-11-21 unless noted. Official sources first; Wikipedia only for general geography.

## The two governments
- The City of Davison is its own city inside the Davison Township survey area; it is administratively separate from the township. Total area 1.98 square miles; 2020 census population 5,143.
  Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Davison,_Michigan (geography only)
- Davison Township: established 1840, about 34 square miles, "located at the border of Genesee and Lapeer Counties". "The I-69 and M-15 corridors allow residents and visitors easy access." "Our blend of commercial, residential, and agricultural zoning." Mentions non-motorized pathways, a Downtown Development District and the Davison Farmers Market. The page gives population 19,575 (2010); Wikipedia gives 20,434 at the 2020 census.
  Sources: https://davisontwp-mi.gov/about-us ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Davison_Township,_Michigan
- Township office: 1280 N. Irish Rd., Davison, MI 48423, (810) 653-4156. City Hall and DPW: 200 E Flint Street, (810) 653-2191.
  Sources: https://davisontwp-mi.gov/trash-and-recycling/ ; https://www.cityofdavisonmi.gov/180/Department-of-Public-Works

## Lot sizes and land (township)
- Davison Township Zoning Ordinance, Article XVI Schedule of Regulations: RC Recreation Conservation 10 acre minimum; RA Residential Agricultural 10 acre minimum (nonfarm lots of 2 acres may be created by a sliding scale); RSE Residential Suburban Estate 1.5 acre minimum, 150 ft width; RU-1 Residential Urban, 1 to 3.2 units per acre.
  Source: https://davisontwp-mi.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/DVT-Zoning-Ordinance-Amended-Thru-16-84-with-Illustrations.pdf (Sections 1600 and 1601)
- What it means: the township runs from subdivision lots to 1.5 acre estate lots to 2 acre and 10 acre rural parcels. Big mowing areas, long drives and road frontage outside the city; compact lots inside it.

## City size and streets
- "The City of Davison's Department of Public Works is responsible for maintaining 5.33 miles of major Streets, 13.24 miles of local streets, and 2.4 miles of State Highway. Maintenance consists of snow removal, road and curb repairs, leaf pickup and street sweeping within the city limits."
  Source: City of Davison, "Snow & Ice Removal Notice", posted 2026-11-13: https://www.cityofdavisonmi.gov/CivicAlerts.aspx?AID=761
- M-15 runs through the city; the city splits its leaf pickup east and west of M-15 (see below).

## Soil (USDA)
- USDA NRCS Soil Data Access, point queries run 2026-11-21 (https://sdmdataaccess.sc.egov.usda.gov):
  - City center (43.0347, -83.5180): Conover loam, 0 to 4 percent slopes, somewhat poorly drained.
  - East side of the city (43.030, -83.505): Conover-Metamora sandy loams, 2 to 6 percent slopes, somewhat poorly drained.
  - Northeast township (43.07, -83.46): Celina loam, 2 to 6 percent slopes, moderately well drained.
  - South of the city (42.99, -83.50): Brookston loam, 0 to 2 percent slopes, poorly drained.
- What it means: loamy soils that stay wet in spring, especially on flat ground. Lawns rut under heavy equipment when soft; beds can sit wet; timing of spring work matters.

## Sidewalk snow (city)
- City Ordinance Section 1024.10, quoted in the city's notice: "(a) All owners or occupants of property adjacent to a public sidewalk shall clear said sidewalk of snow and ice within a reasonable time (considering factors such as, but not limited to, weather, accumulation, and time of day) as determined by the city manager or his/her designee. Failure to clear ... shall cause the city to clear the sidewalk, the cost of which, in addition to a ... administrative fee, shall be placed as a lien upon such premises." The notice says "within a reasonable time (Usually 48 hours) after a snowfall or the formation of ice".
- (b) No draining water or any liquid capable of freezing onto a public sidewalk, street, alley or parking area in November through March.
- (c) "No person shall pile, dump or place snow, ice or any other material on any sidewalk or public street in the city. SNOW OR ICE SHALL NOT BE PLOWED OR MOVED ACROSS A PUBLIC STREET..."
- The notice: "If you have any questions, or are in need of obtaining a list of snow removal companies, please contact City Hall."
  Source: https://www.cityofdavisonmi.gov/CivicAlerts.aspx?AID=761 (posted 2026-11-13)
- Snow emergency parking: no city page found. Left out.

## Michigan law on snow in the road
- MCL 257.677a(4): "A person shall not deposit, or cause to be deposited, snow, ice or slush on any roadway or highway." (2) and (3) also bar removing or depositing snow onto or across a roadway or shoulder in a way that blocks a driver's line of sight.
  Source: https://www.legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-257-677a

## Road Commission (township roads)
- Genesee County Road Commission winter priority: state roads; county primary roads (multi-lane, then two lane); county local paved roads (high, then low traffic); county local roads (unpaved)*; subdivision streets*. "* Road crews do not work overtime on these roads or streets."
- Mailboxes damaged during snow removal are replaced with a standard mailbox and/or post as schedules permit; all roads are cleared before mailbox repairs. FAQ: property owners are encouraged to inspect mailboxes and supports before each winter.
- FAQ also lists decorative timber garden borders, big mailbox posts, rocks and similar items in the right of way as traffic hazards.
  Sources: https://www.gcrc.org/practices ; https://gcrc.org/about/frequently-asked-questions

## Leaves and yard waste
### City of Davison
- DPW fall leaf pickup, 2026 schedule (posted 2026-10-14, "only a guide and subject to change"): three complete pickups, each covering every street. First on or before October 19 citywide; second October 26 east of M-15 and November 2 west of M-15; third November 9; a final pickup if able and needed November 16 to 20; street sweeping the week of November 23. Signs go up the Friday before; residents should avoid parking in the street and rake leaves into the gutter. "ONLY place leaves in the gutter. ROCKS, BOARDS, BRUSH etc. cause damage to the equipment."
  Source: https://www.cityofdavisonmi.gov/CivicAlerts.aspx?AID=757
- Curbside trash, recycling and yard waste: Priority Waste. 2026 calendar: service on Mondays; "Yard Waste is weekly April-November"; first yard waste pickup April 6, last November 30. Yard waste in biodegradable paper leaf bags, clearly marked cans, or bundled; pieces not longer than 4 feet or thicker than 2 inches; bags or cans not over 50 pounds. Out by 7 a.m. or the night before.
  Sources: https://www.cityofdavisonmi.gov/892/Find-the-TrashRecycling-Schedule ; https://www.cityofdavisonmi.gov/DocumentCenter/View/1793
### Davison Township
- Yard waste collection the first Tuesday in April through the last Tuesday in November, weekly. Brown kraft bags or a container marked "Yard Waste" (stickers at the township office). Branches no more than 2 inches thick, tied and bundled, nothing over 4 feet. Under 50 pounds. No stumps or trunks. No plastic bags. Trash weekly on Tuesdays; curb by 7 a.m. Hauler: Emterra.
  Source: https://davisontwp-mi.gov/trash-and-recycling/

## Burning (township)
- Davison Township Open Burning Ordinance (No. 29-1, adopted 2020-02-10): burning allowed with a permit covers "Brush, tree trimmings, shrubbery, and other wood materials" (plus agricultural and forestry practices). Leaves are not on that list. Permit from the Building Department, in person or online, valid for the calendar year. Daylight hours only; constantly attended; not in a road or right of way; not on commercial property or within 50 feet of a structure; no fire in high wind.
  Sources: https://davisontwp-mi.gov/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/burn-p-ord2019.pdf ; https://davisontwp-mi.gov/fillable-forms/burn-permit-application/
- Pages say: check with the township before burning anything; we haul leaves away.

## Grass height rules
- City of Davison, Chapter 656, Section 656.02 (public notice posted 2026-03-31): no grass or rank vegetation over six inches on average, and no noxious weeds, on lots under one acre; on unplatted lots over one acre, within 50 feet of the street or a property line.
  Source: https://www.cityofdavisonmi.gov/CivicAlerts.aspx?AID=682
- Davison Township Ordinance 2010-51-A: owners or occupants must cut weeds and grass as often as needed; in platted subdivisions, vacant lots not next to a dwelling only need the right of way mowed.
  Source: https://davisontwp-mi.gov/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/weeds_ordinance_-2010-51-a.pdf

## General horticulture (MSU Extension), used across pages
- Do not remove more than one third of the leaf blade in one mowing; 2.5 to 3.5 inches is the general height for most lawns. https://www.canr.msu.edu/resources/mowing-lawn-turf
- A mat of leaves left on turf over winter smothers it, slows spring green-up and can mean more snow mold; mulch or remove leaves before snow. https://www.canr.msu.edu/resources/mulch_leaves_into_turf_for_a_smart_lawn
- 2 to 3 inches of mulch is enough; keep it off trunks (no mulch volcanoes). https://www.canr.msu.edu/news/mulching_landscape_beds_faqs ; https://www.canr.msu.edu/news/nightmare_results_for_appliers_of_volcano_mulch
- Spring-flowering shrubs (lilac, forsythia, spirea, weigela) are best pruned right after they bloom; they set next year's buds the summer before. https://www.canr.msu.edu/news/pruning_spring_flowering_shrubs
- Some pollinators shelter in stems and leaf litter into spring; waiting until mid to late April is likely fine for most cavity-nesting bees. https://www.canr.msu.edu/news/when-is-it-safe-to-clean-up-my-yard-this-spring
